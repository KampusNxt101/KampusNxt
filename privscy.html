Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Kampus Nxt, we respect the privacy of students, graduates, institutions, visitors, and other users who interact with our website and Career Readiness Program.

This Privacy Policy explains how Kampus Nxt may collect, use, store, protect, and process personal information when users visit our website, contact us, register for a program, participate in training, or use our services.

By using the Kampus Nxt website or voluntarily providing personal information, users acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. About Kampus Nxt

Kampus Nxt is a Career Readiness Program focused on helping students and graduates improve their preparedness for internships, campus placements, interviews, and early-career opportunities.

Our services may include:

  • Career guidance
  • Resume development
  • LinkedIn profile development
  • GitHub and portfolio guidance
  • Aptitude preparation
  • Coding and technical interview preparation
  • Mock interviews
  • Communication and soft-skills training
  • Industry-oriented projects
  • Mentorship
  • Placement preparation and support
