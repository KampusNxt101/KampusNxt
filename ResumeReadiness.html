Is Your Resume Ready?

Before applying for your next opportunity, make sure your resume can answer these important questions:

  • Does your resume clearly communicate your career objective and professional profile?
  • Are your most relevant skills and technologies easy to identify?
  • Are your projects and experience explained with clear responsibilities and outcomes?
  • Does your resume highlight your achievements instead of only listing duties?
  • Is the content relevant to the job roles you are targeting?
  • Is the overall resume professional, readable, consistent, and ATS-friendly?
