Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KampusNxt may collect, use, store, protect, and process personal information when users visit our website, contact us, register for a program, participate in training, or use our services.

By using the KampusNxt website or voluntarily providing personal information, users acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

KampusNxt is a Career Readiness Program focused on helping students and graduates improve their preparedness for internships, campus placements, interviews, and early-career opportunities.

Our services may include:

    Career guidance
    Resume development
    LinkedIn profile development
    GitHub and portfolio guidance
    Aptitude preparation
    Coding and technical interview preparation
    Mock interviews
    Communication and soft-skills training
    Industry-oriented projects
    Mentorship
    Placement preparation and support
