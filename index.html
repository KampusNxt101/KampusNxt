Every year, thousands of talented engineering students graduate with strong academic knowledge but struggle to secure placements because they lack industry exposure, practical experience, communication skills, and interview confidence.

Our Career Readiness Program was created to solve this challenge by providing a structured roadmap that prepares students for modern industry expectations before graduation.
